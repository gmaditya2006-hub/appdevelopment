Know Your Waste

Learn where everyday items belong.

🍃

Organic Waste

Food and biodegradable materials

  • Fruit & vegetable peels
  • Food leftovers
  • Garden waste
  • Tea & coffee grounds
♻️

Recyclable Waste

Materials that can be processed again

  • Paper & cardboard
  • Plastic bottles
  • Glass
  • Metal cans
💻

E-Waste

Electronic products requiring special handling

  • Phones
  • Chargers
  • Laptops
  • Keyboards
⚠️

Hazardous Waste

Materials requiring careful handling

  • Batteries
  • Chemicals
  • Paint
  • Certain bulbs

Awareness

Why Proper Segregation Matters

🌍

Environmental Pollution

Mixed waste contaminates soil and air, harming ecosystems and biodiversity.

🔥

Global Warming

Organic waste in landfills releases methane, accelerating climate change.

🏥

Health Issues

Improper handling of hazardous materials can cause serious illnesses and injuries.

🌊

Marine Life

Plastic and chemical waste can end up in rivers and oceans, endangering species.

💰

Economic Impact

Unsorted waste increases cleanup costs and reduces recycling recovery value.
